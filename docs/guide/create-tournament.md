# Create a Tournament

PointIQ organizes a competition as a tournament with team slots and matches. In `Squad` mode, enter each squad as a team name in its team slot.

## Create the tournament

1. From `Tournament Control`, tap `New Tournament`.
2. On `Create Tournament`, enter a `Tournament Name`.
3. In `Select your game`, use `Free Fire MAX`.
4. In `Mode`, use `Squad`.
5. If you are using a group rotation, turn on `Group Rotation Format`. Select `3 Groups` or `4 Groups`, then select at least one group pairing. If you are not using group rotation, leave this option off.
6. Tap `Create Tournament`.

PointIQ opens the tournament details and then takes you to team entry. You may need to sign in before creating a tournament. An account can have up to five tournaments; delete an existing tournament before creating another after reaching that limit.

## Enter squad names

### Standard tournament

1. On `Enter Teams`, enter the first squad in the `Slot 1` field.
2. Continue in slot order. Do not leave an earlier slot empty while entering a later slot.
3. To enter several names at once, tap `Paste List`, enter one team name per line in `Paste Team List`, and tap `Apply`. The paste action accepts up to 12 team names.
4. Tap `Save team names`.

### Group Rotation tournament

1. On the group rotation team-entry screen, select a group pairing.
2. Enter the teams by their local lobby position for that pairing. Each pairing requires all 12 lobby rows to be filled.
3. Do not enter the same team twice within one pairing.
4. Save the team names, then repeat the process for each selected pairing.

You can return to the tournament details and tap `Edit Teams` to change the entered team names.

## Create or continue a match

1. Return to `Tournament Details`.
2. In the `Calculate Points` or `MATCHES` area, tap the next match action. A standard tournament shows an action such as `Match 1`; a group rotation tournament shows an action such as `Add Match 1` under a group-pairing section.
3. On `Create match`, enter a whole-number `Match number` from 1 to 18, choose the `Match date` with `Select date`, and enter the `Map`.
4. Tap `Create match`.
5. Complete the placement and kills steps that follow. After they are saved, PointIQ opens `Match Review`, where you can add the match screenshots.

Existing matches can be opened from the `MATCHES` area by tapping their `Match 1`, `Match 2`, or similar match entry.

PointIQ requires the tournament roster to be confirmed before a match can be created. It also prevents duplicate match numbers and limits a tournament to 18 matches.

When Match Review opens, continue with [Add Screenshots](/guide/add-screenshots).
