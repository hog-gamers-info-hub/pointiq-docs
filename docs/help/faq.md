# Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about PointIQ.

## General

### What is PointIQ?

PointIQ is an Android application for managing Free Fire and Free Fire MAX tournament results. It helps tournament organizers calculate match results, manage standings, and create downloadable result tables.

### Is PointIQ free?

Yes. PointIQ is free to use, without subscriptions or advertisements.

### Where can I download PointIQ?

Download PointIQ from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pointiq.app).

### Can I create tournaments or calculate results on this website?

No. This website provides official documentation, support information, and links. Tournament creation, result calculation, and exports take place inside the PointIQ Android application.

## Result Calculation

### How does automatic result calculation work?

PointIQ reads match result screenshots using OCR and processes the recognized information into calculated results for review.

See [Automatic Calculation](/guide/auto-calculate).

### Do I need result screenshots for automatic calculation?

Yes. Automatic result calculation requires appropriate result screenshots. If they are missing, PointIQ prompts you to add them.

### Can I enter results manually?

Yes. PointIQ supports manual result entry when you prefer not to use automatic calculation.

See [Manual Result Entry](/guide/manual-results).

### Is automatic result calculation always accurate?

No OCR system can guarantee perfect recognition. Screenshot quality, cropping, text recognition, and squad matching can affect the calculated result.

Always review the calculated values before finalizing a match.

### What if PointIQ recognizes the wrong squad or elimination count?

Review the calculated results and correct supported values before finalization.

See [Review and Finalize](/guide/review-finalize).

## Finalization and Standings

### Can I edit a match after finalization?

Finalized match results are normally read-only. Review all values carefully before finalizing.

### How are tournament standings calculated?

PointIQ calculates tournament standings from finalized match results.

See [Tournament Standings](/guide/standings).

### What happens when teams have equal total points?

PointIQ uses these tie-breaking rules in order:

1. Total points.
2. Total kills.
3. Number of Booyahs.
4. Total placement points.

### Why can total points differ from kills plus placement points?

Bonus or penalty adjustments may affect the final total.

Review the match result details to understand the adjustments.

## Screenshots and Data

### Will my screenshots always remain available?

Screenshot availability depends on how the images are stored and recovered. Do not assume that all screenshots will remain accessible after clearing app data, reinstalling the app, or restoring a tournament.

### Why does a finalized match show "Screenshots unavailable"?

The screenshot files may no longer be accessible even though the finalized result data is still available.

See [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting).

### Can I recover missing screenshots?

Recovery is not guaranteed. If the screenshots are unavailable, contact PointIQ support for assistance with your specific situation.
