# Contact & Support

Need help with PointIQ? Contact our team, report problems, or connect with our official community.

## Email Support

For application issues, general questions, or business inquiries:

**Email:** [team@point-iq.com](mailto:team@point-iq.com)

When reporting a technical problem, please include the following information where available:

- The PointIQ app version.
- A brief description of the problem.
- Steps to reproduce the issue.
- A relevant screenshot, if appropriate.

Never share passwords, verification codes, or sensitive account information.
